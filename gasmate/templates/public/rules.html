LPG Safety Rules

  • Always store LPG cylinders in an upright position in a well-ventilated area.
  • Never use a cylinder that is damaged, dented, or has a leaking valve.
  • Turn off the regulator knob after every use.
  • Keep cylinders away from heat sources and open flames.
  • Never attempt to repair or modify cylinders or regulators yourself.
  • In case of a gas leak, do not switch on/off any electrical appliances — ventilate the area immediately.
  • Keep a fire extinguisher nearby in the kitchen.
  • Ensure the rubber hose is ISI certified and replaced every 2 years.
