Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a gas cylinder?

Log in to your customer account and go to Dashboard → Book Cylinder. Select your cylinder type and confirm.

How often can I book a cylinder?

You can book a cylinder once every 20 days per cylinder type.

How do I apply for a new gas connection?

Click "Apply Online" in the navigation and fill in your details along with a valid ID proof.

How do I change my dealer?

Log in and go to Dashboard → Change Location. Select your new city and preferred dealer.

Can I order accessories?

Yes! Log in and visit Dashboard → Order Accessories to browse and order items like stoves, regulators, and hoses.

How do I end my gas service?

Log in and go to Dashboard → End Service. Submit a reason and our team will process your request.

How do I track my order?

Visit Dashboard → My Orders to see all cylinder and accessory orders along with their current status.
